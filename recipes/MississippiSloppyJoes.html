Mississippi Sloppy Joes

These Mississippi sloppy joes combine the bold, tangy flavors of Mississippi pot roast with the ease of classic sloppy joes. Made with ground beef, ranch seasoning, au jus mix, onion, and pepperoncini peppers, they’re a quick, family-friendly dinner everyone will love.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet ranch seasoning
  • 1 packet au jus mix
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1/2 cup pepperoncini peppers

Instructions

  1. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned. Drain excess fat.
  2. Add the diced onion and cook until softened.
  3. Stir in the ranch seasoning, au jus mix, and pepperoncini peppers. Simmer for 5-10 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together.
  4. Serve the sloppy joe mixture on hamburger buns and enjoy!
