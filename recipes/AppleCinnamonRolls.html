Apple Cinnamon Rolls Recipe

These apple “cider bath” cinnamon rolls are a seasonal twist on cream-bathed cinnamon rolls. I used apple cider to bathe the rolls and the results were terrific. In addition I am using a very easy no knead dough for my cinnamon rolls.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup warm milk (110 degrees F/45 degrees C)
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1/3 cup butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons ground cinnamon
  • 1/3 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup apple cider
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, dissolve yeast in warm milk. Add sugar, butter, salt, eggs and flour. Mix well. Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled in size, about 1 hour.
  2. Roll dough out into a rectangle. Spread softened butter over the dough. Mix brown sugar and cinnamon together and sprinkle over the butter. Roll up dough and cut into 12 rolls. Place rolls in a greased 9x13 inch baking dish. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 30 minutes. Preheat oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  3. Bake rolls in preheated oven until golden brown, about 20 minutes. While rolls are baking, mix apple cider, powdered sugar, and vanilla extract together in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil and cook for 1 minute. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.
  4. Remove rolls from oven and pour apple cider glaze over the rolls. Let cool for 10 minutes before serving.
